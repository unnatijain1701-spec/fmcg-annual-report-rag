## What does Nestle India say about commodity and milk price inflation?


Nestle India reports the following regarding commodity and milk price inflation:

**Commodity Price Inflation**
*   **Impacts and Risks:** Inflation is noted as a general risk that can adversely affect profit margins due to rising raw material costs and impact consumer buying power [Nestle India | FY2023-24 | Risk]. The company has specifically noted volatile commodity prices and escalating food inflation in coffee and cocoa [Nestle India | FY2023-24 | Strategy]. Additionally, volatile food prices have been linked to erratic monsoon rains in India and climate change [Nestle India | CY2022 | Risk].
*   **Trends by Commodity (FY2025-26):**
    *   **Edible oil:** Prices have moved higher and are firm, supported by global crude oil prices and increased diversion to biodiesel [Nestle India | FY2025-26 | MD&A].
    *   **Wheat:** Lower quality and quantity, as well as delayed harvests, resulted from unseasonal rains in April [Nestle India | FY2025-26 | MD&A].
    *   **Cocoa and Sugar:** Sugar prices remain stable and cocoa prices remain subdued due to moderated demand and improved supply [Nestle India | FY2025-26 | MD&A].
    *   **Milk:** Prices have firmed [Nestle India | FY2025-26 | MD&A].
*   **Mitigation Strategies:** 
    *   The company utilizes a comprehensive procurement process involving the identification of potential fluctuations and continuous monitoring of price indices [Nestle India | FY2023-24 | Risk], [Nestle India | FY2024-25 | Risk], [Nestle India | CY2022 | Risk].
